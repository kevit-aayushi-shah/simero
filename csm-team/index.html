Pillar 1 Onboarding

You get full ownership of the onboarding.

Netcore

Find the root cause early and escalate fast

  • Ask for everything you need up front so follow-ups stay low.
  • Decide early whether the issue is on our side or Netcore's.
  • Escalate to the manager or Netcore team as soon as the blocker is clear.
Chatomate

Run the onboarding call on your own

  • Onboard through the admin panel and embedded signup.
  • Lead the call yourself, without the manager.
  • Leave the client with a working account and clear next steps.

Pillar 5 Initiatives

Credit for the work nobody asked you to do.

What counts

  • A new use case or upsell idea pitched to a client
  • A template or checklist the team can reuse
  • A quick fix or automation that saves time
  • A product improvement raised with evidence

How it is measured

  • Initiatives logged per month
  • How many came from you, not from an assignment
  • Quick wins delivered within the same week
Pillar 6 Feature knowledge

Know every Chatomate feature. Your answer must be right.

If you say "yes, it's available"

It must exist and work the way you described. A wrong yes counts as a mistake.

If you say "no"

Escalate it to the manager as a possible product gap, with the client's exact requirement.

Easy

Standard features. Answer and set up on your own, immediately.

Medium

Needs setup or integration knowledge. Answer correctly, set up with docs.

Hard

Advanced, API or custom logic. Know what is possible, escalate details.

1st time

The manager explains the product gap in full.

2nd time

Explained again. Take notes.

3rd time · last

After this you answer it yourself.
